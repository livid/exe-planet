# Ivory

A site template for [Planet](https://github.com/Planetable/Planet): an ivory page beside a panel that holds the site's avatar, large, and its name. It is the look of the [Planet](https://www.planetable.xyz/) app's own website of 2022, made for a product with guides.

The page has a row of links at the top: Home and the site's pages in the navigation. A small line at the foot links the archive, the tags when any post has some, and the feed. On a phone the panel sits above the page.

The home page shows the site's About in full. Write the About in Markdown: it can hold headings, figures, pictures and HTML, so it can be the product's front page. Under it come the posts, unless the setting Posts on the Home Page is Hide: then the home page is the About alone, for a site whose pages lead to its posts (a Guides page, say, with its own list).

A post has its title, its date and its text; a page has its title and text. A text with two headings or more gets a table of contents above it, made by a small script from its headings, and a long page gets a button back to the top.

It reads the same context as the Plain template, so it builds a paginated index, posts, pages, the archive, the tag list and tag pages.

It comes from [exe](https://exe.v2core.com/).
