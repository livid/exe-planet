The button is a Platinum window. Every page of blog.v2core.com is a Mac OS 9 window on the desk gray, so I made the banner one more of them: a tiny active window titled "exe-planet", its content a patch of space with the Planet app's ringed globe in the middle. A reader who knows the blog knows the title bar; anyone else still reads a name and a planet.

Row 0 and the outer columns are the 1px #262626 window edge. Row 1 and column 1 are the white top-left bevel, and column 86 is the #999 shade. Rows 2 to 10 are the title bar: a 7x7 close box at x4 (the desktop's 13px box shrunk to a sunken ring, the black ring and a raised centre), stripes on rows 3 to 8, and the title on a #ccc field. The title is centred in the room right of the close box, which is where the desktop's flex row puts it. Row 11 is the single black seam. Rows 12 to 29 are the content, 86x18 of #333399, and the 26x16 planet sits dead centre with one pixel of space above and below.

The palette is 13 colours, all borrowed. The greys (#ccc body, #999 bevel, #777 and white stripe rows), the #333399 highlight and the #ccf lavender come from the desktop's chrome CSS. The globe, cap, land and ring colours come from the Planet icon.svg, except that I swapped the icon's pure black for the Platinum #262626 so there is only one black. The planet is the icon redrawn at 16px, not scaled: the lit cap, three white highlight dashes, two continents with lit edges and an island, and a ring that runs behind the globe above the middle and in front below, with the icon's shade at the band's ends.

The lettering is a bold face in the manner of Chicago and Charcoal, since Platinum titles are bold: 2px stems, 1px horizontals, a 5px x-height, 2px ascender on l, 1px on t, and 1px of tracking. It comes to 54px. The p gets a single descender row, because a second one would have touched the seam; at 1x it still reads as p.

Two stars twinkle in turn, one each side of the planet: dim lavender dot, white dot, a plus-shaped sparkle with lavender arms, then back. That's eight frames of 300ms, looping. Everything else holds still. I didn't spin the globe: at 16px, turning it smoothly needs about twenty frames, and with eight the land jumps. Delta frames keep the file near a kilobyte.

From the classic 88x31 I kept the full rectangle, the 1px border, the bevel and the dense, readable-at-1x feel. I rejected a hard window shadow (it would cut two corners out of the rectangle), a grow box (a fixed-size window needs none), gradients and any anti-aliasing. I also dropped the icon's white pixel inside the ring, which read as a stray star on the dark blue.

Three candidates lost. A button badge with the planet at left and the name at right was clean but generic. The bold name crowded its right bevel, and a light 1px face that fit looked thin next to the planet. A split badge put the planet on a starry panel and "exe" over "planet" in a 6px Chicago-like face. It had the strongest type, but it lost the hyphen, and its two panels read as two things. An earlier version of this window with the light face in the title bar lost the Platinum weight that makes the bar recognisable.
